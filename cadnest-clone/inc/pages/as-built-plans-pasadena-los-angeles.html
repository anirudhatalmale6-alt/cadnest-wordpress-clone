What Are As-Built Plans?

As-built plans document the current condition of a structure based on field measurements and site observations. They show how a building exists today, which is essential when preparing permit drawings, correcting previous undocumented work or planning new construction.

As-built drawings are commonly used for:

Remodel and renovation planning

Room additions and expansions

Garage conversion ADUs

Real estate property documentation

Permit corrections

Existing condition verification

Unpermitted structure documentation
